## 登录配置
### AppGallery Connect 配置Google认证
登录 [Google云平台](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials?project=fishing-game-zone&hl=fi&supportedpurview=project)，位置：**凭据 > OAuth2.0客户端ID > web应用的客户端ID**，复制信息。

![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733705467063.png)
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733705899425.png)
firebase后台配置google登录，一般会自动生成客户端ID。
![img_v3_02ga_e9e92955-2610-4b68-a124-da9519e92dcg.jpg](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/img_v3_02ga_e9e92955-2610-4b68-a124-da9519e92dcg-1730708770359.jpg)
2. 开启Google权限认证，**位置：AppGallery Connect >My projects>Auth Service>Google** 将从google云平台获取的参数配置到如下图。`Client ID`为Google的`客户端 ID`,`Client secret`填写`客户端密钥`
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733705802972.png)
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733705825053.png)
### AppGallery Connect 获取密钥

1. 登录 [AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/)，位置：**AppGallery Connect >Project settings>General information**。
2. 获取关键配置：`Project ID`
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689162101.png)
3. 获取`客户端ID`、`客户端密钥`,位置：**AppGallery Connect >Project settings>Server SDK**
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689320979.png)
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689420662.png)
### 望舒系统中配置登录信息

1. 复制到望舒系统，位置：**企业管理后台 > 商务管理  > 渠道包管理 > 添加登录方式**。
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733549126906.png)
