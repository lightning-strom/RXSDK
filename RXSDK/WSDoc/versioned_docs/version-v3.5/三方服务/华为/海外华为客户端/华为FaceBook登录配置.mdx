## 准备工作

::: tip
AppGallery Connect：[https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/)

## 登录配置
### AppGallery Connect 配置FaceBook认证
1.登录FaceBook开发者后台，位置：**应用设置>基本/高级** 获取`应用编号`和`客户端口令`
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733561787466.png)
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733561823130.png)
2. 开启FaceBook权限认证，**位置：AppGallery Connect >My projects>Auth Service>FaceBook** 将从FaceBook开发者后台获取的参数配置到如下图。`AppId`为FaceBook的`应用编号`,`App Secret`填写`客户端口令`
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733561497074.png)
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1733561621817.png)
### AppGallery Connect 获取密钥

1. 登录 [AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/)，位置：**AppGallery Connect >Project settings>General information**。
2. 获取关键配置：`Project ID`
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689162101.png)
3. 获取`客户端ID`、`客户端密钥`,位置：**AppGallery Connect >Project settings>Server SDK**
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689320979.png)
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689420662.png)
### 望舒系统中配置登录信息

1. 复制到望舒系统，位置：**企业管理后台 > 商务管理  > 渠道包管理 > 添加登录方式**。
![image.png](https://haiqi-ruixue-test.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/docOnline/image-1732689476698.png)
